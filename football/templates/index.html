An open-source framework for analyzing NFL player projection accuracy

Drops applies the Whiffs evaluation framework to football: score counting stats per game, adjust for league average, and weight errors by games played. (See the methodology.)

Passing, rushing, and receiving are scored as per-game rates (attempts, yards, touchdowns, and the rest). Projection systems in football/projection_evaluation.py are scored when their CSVs line up with a season of actuals.
